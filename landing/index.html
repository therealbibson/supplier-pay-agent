Celo · Agents Open for Business — Stablecoin Corridors

Pay overseas suppliers in their local stablecoin.

PayBridge is an agent that gets a Textile FX quote and pays an overseas supplier in their local stablecoin on Celo.

View on GitHub ↗ See live proof

How it works

  1. 1

    Get a quote

    The agent requests a Textile FX quote — USD₹ → cNGN and other corridors.

  2. 2

    Swap on Celo

    The swap runs on Celo mainnet, with the attribution tag on-chain.

  3. 3

    Supplier paid

    The supplier receives payment in their local stablecoin.

Corridors: USD₹ ↔ cNGN, IDRX, wBRL, wARS, wMXN, wCOP.
